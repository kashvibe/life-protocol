Daily Breakfast & Snacks

Fresh components assembled on the day to maintain quality.

🍳

Breakfast Box

750ml Container • Eat at 9:15 AM

  • 1. Spray pan with 5g olive oil.
  • 2. Fry 2 Large Eggs completely through.
  • 3. Toss 100g cherry tomatoes and 50g baby spinach in pan until wilted.
  • 4. Pack alongside 2 slices wholemeal toast.
💡 Cook fresh daily or the night before. Eat cold or lightly warmed.
☕

Afternoon Snack

750ml Container • Assemble at Work

  • 1. Weigh 80g raw rolled oats into container.
  • 2. Add boiling water from kettle. Let sit for 3 mins.
  • 3. Stir in exactly 15g Collagen Powder & cinnamon.
  • 4. Top with 1 sliced banana (100g).

The Weekend Protocol

Zero-Effort Strategy

Keep your cooking zero-effort on weekends so you can stay laid back while hitting exact macro splits.

🥪

1. The Office Press Hack

Use your work sandwich press at home, lined with greaseproof baking paper. Grill 180g plain chicken thighs or 100g paneer fresh.

2. Microwave Sides

Pair your protein with a quick microwave-steamed steam-bag of broccoli and 150g of pre-packaged microwave brown rice.
